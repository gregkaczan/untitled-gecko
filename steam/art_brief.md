# Underhum: Steam capsule art brief

The minimum art for a public Coming Soon page on Steam: four store capsules.
They come from two pieces, a logo and one key art illustration, each capsule
laid out by the artist for its own shape.

## The game

A small gecko in a dark cave where every grain is simulated: sand pours,
boulders roll, water flows. The gecko climbs walls and ceilings, chews through
rock, swings on its tongue and squeezes through cracks. Glowing slugs and
mushrooms light the dark. The game is pixel art: one art pixel is 4 screen
pixels.

## Style

Open: painted, hand drawn or pixel art. It need not match the game's pixels.
It should feel like the same world: a dark cave, green glow from slugs and
mushrooms, the gecko's slim body and long tail, falling sand. A hand-drawn
piece reads better at Steam's small sizes; pixel art should then use a finer
grid than the game's 4 px.

## Deliverables

1. **Logo.** The word "Underhum", one capital U. Transparent background.
   It must stay readable at 120 x 45 px, the smallest size Steam shows.
   A version for a dark background is enough; the art is dark.
2. **Key art.** The gecko in the cave. Paint it big enough for every crop
   below, and keep the layers apart: background, gecko, foreground and light,
   logo. One layered file (PSD, Aseprite or Krita) plus flat PNGs.
3. **The four capsules** in the table below, at the exact sizes, each laid
   out for its shape: the logo placed and sized per capsule, the scene
   rearranged where a crop would not fit (the vertical one above all).
   Checked at 100% and at the small sizes Steam shrinks them to.
4. Later, before release, the library set in the same way (see below).

## Files

- **Key art:** a layered PSD (Krita and Aseprite both save one), at least
  3840 x 2160, sRGB, 8 bits a channel, layers named. Plus a flat PNG of the
  whole piece and a PNG of each main layer with transparency.
- **Logo:** vector (SVG or PDF) if it is lettered as vector; otherwise a PNG
  at least 2560 px wide. Transparent background. One light version for dark
  art; a dark version too if easy.
- **Pixel art:** the native-size PNG and the Aseprite file; no scaling on
  export, the crops are scaled by whole steps afterwards.
- **Capsules:** PNG at the exact pixel sizes in the table, sRGB, no
  transparency. Steam takes PNG or JPG; PNG keeps the edges clean.

## Rights

The art is used on Steam, the game, trailers and other marketing. Agree on
that in writing before work starts. Say whether any AI tool was used: Steam's
content survey asks.

## The crops

| Capsule | Pixels | Shape | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | 920 x 430 | 2.1 : 1 | Top of the store page. Logo plus art. |
| Main | 1232 x 706 | 1.75 : 1 | Front page features. Logo plus art. |
| Small | 462 x 174 | 2.7 : 1 | Search lists. The logo nearly fills it. |
| Vertical | 748 x 896 | 0.83 : 1 | Seasonal sales. Logo plus art. |

A wide canvas of 3840 x 2160 or more, with the gecko near the middle and room
above and below it, gives material for all four. The library set needed later
before release: 3840 x 1240 without any text, keeping the middle 860 x 380
clear for cropping; 600 x 900 portrait; 920 x 430; and the logo alone as a
transparent PNG 1280 wide or 720 tall.

## Steam's rules for capsules

- No text except the game's title. No slogans, quotes, awards or "coming soon".
- The logo must be readable against the background.
- Show what the game is: the gecko, the cave, the glow.
