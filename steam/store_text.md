# Underhum: Steam store text (draft)

A first draft for the store page fields. Valve's review rule: list only what
the game has at launch, or mark future features as planned. Cut anything below
that does not ship.

## Name

Underhum

## Short description

Steam shows this under the header capsule. Keep it under 300 characters.

> A small gecko in a cave where every grain is simulated. Climb walls and
> ceilings, chew through rock, swing on your tongue and squeeze through cracks
> while sand pours, boulders roll and water finds its way down.

## About this game

Paste into the Description tab. Valve does not allow links to other websites
in it.

> You are a gecko, newly hatched in the dark. The cave around you is not a
> backdrop: every grain of sand, every stone and every drop of water is a
> particle that falls, flows and piles up.
>
> **Every surface is a floor.** Walk up walls and along the ceiling. Squeeze
> your body through cracks barely wider than your head. Swim, and climb out
> dripping.
>
> **A tongue for everything.** Tap to strike, hold to grapple across a gap,
> drag a stone out of the way, or catch a glowing slug and swallow it.
>
> **Dig your own way.** Chew tunnels through soft rock. Bite into a sand bed
> and watch it pour. Roll a boulder into a pit and walk across it.
>
> **A cave that answers back.** Moss you slip off, rock that crumbles under
> your feet, gas pockets that burst, currents that carry you.

## Basic info

## Content survey: generative AI

Steam asks about AI-made content that ships with the game. Pre-generated
content counts. Suggested answer:

> Some decor sprites (mushrooms, plants, vines and moss) began as images made
> with Retro Diffusion, an AI pixel-art tool. They were then cleaned,
> recoloured to the game's palette and cut into pieces with our own tools. No
> content is generated by AI while the game runs.

Before you submit, check whether the gecko's own sprites in `decor/gecko/`
also came from Retro Diffusion, and add them if so. The sounds are
synthesised by `tools/gen_sounds.py`, which is not AI.
